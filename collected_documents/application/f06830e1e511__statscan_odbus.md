# Statistics Canada ODBus Access Decision

## Decision

- Access state: `approved`
- Permitted use: licensed bulk download
- Intended role: historical entity-resolution baseline
- Current-status evidence: prohibited

## Licence

Statistics Canada Open Licence.

Required attribution:

`Statistics Canada, Open Database of Businesses, 2023`

## Vintage limitations

The source records were collected from May through December 2022
and released in November 2023.

A matching ODBus record does not prove that a business currently
exists, remains at that address, occupies the building or has a
commercial real-estate requirement.

All current-status claims require newer independent evidence.
