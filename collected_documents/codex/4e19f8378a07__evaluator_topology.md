# Evaluator Topology

## Public layer

Repository-visible schemas, unit/integration/property tests, deterministic
fixtures, replay, structural linting, baselines, and acceptance commands.

## Sealed layer

Protected adversarial cases and known-bad implementations. The builder can
receive pass/fail diagnostics but cannot edit the corpus during its task.

## External hidden layer

Cases maintained outside the builder's context/repository by a separate owner
or platform. This layer is required when overfitting or evaluator gaming could
materially affect the claim.

## Ownership

- evaluator author/approver and implementation builder are separate roles;
- protected paths are enforced mechanically;
- evaluator changes require their own task and independent review;
- the holdout is never generated by the same task it evaluates;
- known-bad cases must fail before a builder is allowed to target the test.
