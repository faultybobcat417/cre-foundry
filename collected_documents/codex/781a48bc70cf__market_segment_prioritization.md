## Provisional first segment

**Brampton and Mississauga industrial, warehouse, flex and light-manufacturing
establishments**, prioritizing manufacturing, logistics/warehousing,
wholesale/import distribution, food processing and regulated health-product
operations.

Initial event modules:

- expansion;
- relocation and renewal;
- occupancy transition;
- capacity or use constraint.

## Why this is a research priority

The segment has multiple independent source families: municipal permits and
applications, federal contracts/grants, import records, hiring data and
regulated-facility records. It also produces concrete field questions about
loading, yard, storage, utilities, process compliance and labour access.

## What is not selected first

Generic distress is a competing risk or specialist queue, not the primary
positive lead module. Central Toronto is valuable for deep historical
reconstruction but adds route complexity. Hamilton is retained for later
specialized and transfer validation.

## Status

Provisionally supported. The segment is rejected if it cannot produce an
audited eligible universe, acceptable historical event rate, sufficient entity
accuracy, lawful sustainable sources or operationally feasible routes.
