# Authorized Acquisition and Execution Fallback Ladder

Use the highest-quality authorized method that works.

## Source acquisition

1. official bulk file or documented API;
2. licensed provider API/export;
3. official open-data catalogue/resource URL;
4. authorized network/DOM extraction;
5. governed browser/computer-vision capture when APIs/DOM are unavailable;
6. user/firm-provided export;
7. manual representative sample and adjudication;
8. synthetic fixture for software behavior only;
9. block and replace the source.

Every step preserves terms, authentication, rate controls, raw bytes, timestamps,
screenshots/logs where relevant, and replay.

Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, paywalls, authorization, robots/source terms,
rate limits, privacy controls, or technical access controls.

## Missing labels

1. authoritative CRM/calendar/transaction event;
2. independent evidence reconciliation;
3. blind manual adjudication;
4. mature-negative/censoring framework;
5. clearly labelled proxy for research only;
6. prospective shadow capture;
7. randomized field collection.

## Missing firm inputs

Generate a structured template and default only the software interface—not the
business fact. Keep dependent live actions disabled.

## Missing route service

Use cached authorized matrices, an approved provider, self-hosted open routing,
then haversine only for synthetic/offline planning. Never claim real route-time
performance from straight-line distance.

## Missing external hidden evaluator

Use public and sealed tests now, register the external owner/interface, and cap
the claim until independent holdout results exist.
