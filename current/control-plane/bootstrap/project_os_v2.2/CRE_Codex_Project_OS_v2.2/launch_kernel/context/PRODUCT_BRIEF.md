# Product Brief

The product is a decision system for Southern Ontario commercial-real-estate
field prospecting.

Its daily output is ten ordered physical business locations for a specific
representative and route-day, with explanations, evidence, visit objectives,
route sequence, access/protection state, and structured field capture.

The system must improve incremental F9 bookings and expected commercial value,
not merely predict generic business change.

Provisional first segment:

- Brampton and Mississauga;
- industrial, warehouse, flex, and light manufacturing;
- manufacturing, logistics, wholesale/import distribution, food processing,
  and regulated health-product establishments.

The segment is a testable starting point, not permanent truth.

The platform separates source acquisition, identity, candidate universe,
point-in-time features, models, economic policy, list composition, routing,
field operation, outcomes, experiments, governance, and replay.

The strongest immediate learning path is an early integrated shadow slice,
then real source/entity/history proof, then spatial/representative calibration,
then randomized route-day testing.
