# ADVERSARIAL-001 material connected campaign

This synthetic, non-influencing layer connects malformed-input controls,
temporal leakage prevention, immutable source state, identity protection,
uncertainty abstention, exact-ten routing, outcome attribution, economic
binding, security controls, idempotent fault behavior, restore, rollback,
and metamorphic properties.

JSON parsing rejects duplicate keys and malformed documents. Features and
source snapshots cannot postdate the decision. Protected or ambiguous
identity cannot clear. High uncertainty or explicit abstention prevents
issuance.

Routes require exactly ten unique, unprotected, feasible locations.
Booking identities are deduplicated before attribution and economic use.
Sensitive or protected details remain outside general logs, and live
authority remains disabled.

Same-key retries are idempotent and conflicting payloads fail closed.
Backup and restored content use canonical SHA-256 identities, and rollback
must restore the prior accepted version.

Metamorphic checks require route-set permutation invariance, deterministic
replay, and monotonic protection behavior. Hard-invariant survivors are
prohibited.

The material implementation is independent of the frozen ADVERSARIAL-001
evaluator and the legacy adversarial campaign. Public proof level 4
establishes synthetic connected-system conformance only; it does not
establish sealed evaluation, hidden-holdout performance, production
security, field performance, or deployment readiness.
